About

About OptimalPrint

The engineering and science behind the calculations

How settings are calculated

Layer height is constrained to 25–75% of nozzle diameter for proper adhesion. Speed is adjusted per printer kinematics — CoreXY handles higher accelerations than bed slingers. The quality slider interpolates between minimum and maximum layer heights with a corresponding speed correction.

Failure risk prediction

Warping risk uses coefficient of thermal expansion data per material. Stringing risk is based on melt viscosity at print temperature. Adhesion risk combines bed temperature requirements with material crystallinity and surface energy characteristics.

Print time estimation

Uses a volumetric flow model: volume estimated from size category and infill. Flow rate = speed × nozzle diameter × layer height × 60. A 1.35× factor accounts for travel moves and acceleration ramps.

Smart manufacturing connection

FDM is a core additive manufacturing process used in smart factories. Process parameter optimisation — exactly what this tool does — reduces waste, energy consumption, and failure rates, directly supporting sustainable manufacturing and Industry 4.0 goals.
